THE SURVEY

POP is a piggyback pulsar survey conducted by the FAST science group. It searches drift-scan observations for pulsars, intermittent emitters and fast radio bursts while making the resulting catalogue useful to the wider pulsar community.

We use single-pulse searches for long-period pulsars, RRATs and FRBs; FFT searches for millisecond pulsars and binary pulsars; and FFA searches for intermediate- and long-period pulsars.

The tables below list publicly released discoveries. Records are updated after scientific review; source-level FRB entries retain their individual burst detections.

Field Glossary FAQ

  • DM: Dispersion Measure, unit pc cm⁻³, total interstellar electron column density
  • FRB Bursts: Count of detected individual burst events for repeating sources
  • PSR DC Name: Internal drift-scan candidate identifier
  • Period: Pulsar spin period in seconds; blank = unconfirmed timing candidate
